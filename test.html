BEHIND THE NAME

The name REGLES is derived from the French word ‘règles’, meaning rules, standards, and precision. It signifies the core principles that govern athletic discipline, tactical mastery, and fair play.

Our Story

  • REGLES was founded in Malaysia with a vision to revolutionize sportswear manufacturing. Our journey began with a commitment to high-performance fabrics, tactical precision, and exceptional craftsmanship.

  • As demand grew across athletic clubs and corporate teams, REGLES expanded operations to include state-of-the-art sublimation printing and custom apparel engineering to meet all client specifications.

  • By combining cutting-edge textile technology with modern aesthetic design, we quickly became a trusted partner for teams, corporate organizations, and athletes seeking premium performance wear.

  • Over the years, REGLES has evolved into a leading sportswear manufacturer in Malaysia, recognized for its uncompromised quality, tactical discipline, and relentless continuous innovation.

Our Vision

To become a trusted leader in custom sublimation apparel by delivering exceptional quality, innovative designs, and outstanding customer satisfaction.

Our Mission

To provide quality custom sublimation apparel while giving back to the community and creating a positive impact through meaningful contributions.

Privacy Policy

At REGLES Apparel Sdn Bhd, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you share with us.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us when making custom order inquiries, including your name, phone number, email address, and order design details.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your details are strictly used to process custom sportswear orders, provide design mockups, update order status via WhatsApp, and deliver your products.

3. Data Protection

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

1. Custom Artwork Approval

All custom jersey designs, names, and numbers must be thoroughly reviewed and approved in writing prior to production start.

2. Production & Delivery

Standard custom sublimation production times apply from the date of deposit and final mockup confirmation.

3. Returns & Replacements

Due to the customized nature of sublimated sportswear, returns are only accepted for manufacturing defects verified upon delivery.
